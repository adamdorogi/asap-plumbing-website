# Grounded visual systems

Mechanism: customers contact a real local plumber directly and see the work behind the promise.
Audience scene: a Brisbane homeowner at home, often on a phone, needing a repair or planning a bathroom.
Cultural home: Brisbane's practical small-trade businesses, bright subtropical light, local renovations and workshop signage.

Category rut: blue contractor template with stock plumber, stars and equal icon cards. Predictable opposite: luxury beige interiors without the person doing the work.

1. Painted workshop fascia: bold condensed orange and black lettering, plain white working surfaces, large direct phone actions; establishes clarity and a real local personality.
2. Queensland renovation journal: generous photographic project spreads, job-specific captions and sunlight; establishes care through actual work.
3. Service-van identity: highly visible orange panels, cropped oversized lettering, immediate practical contact; reads quickly from a distance.
4. Community sporting-club programme: compact local identity, large service headings, approachable face and suburb names; familiar independent-business confidence.
5. Renovation material sample board: contrasting white ceramic, brushed steel and orange marker accents; lets fixtures and craftsmanship govern the structure.
6. Hardware counter order slip: structured service rows and a useful job enquiry form, direct and task-oriented; makes the next step tangible.
7. Brisbane wayfinding system: confident directions, neighbourhood geography, simple functional sign blocks; situates the business and action.

These candidates span signage, publishing, identity, community print, physical material and transactional systems. Existing orange/black brand commitments must survive any assigned form.
