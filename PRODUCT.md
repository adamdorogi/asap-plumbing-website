# ASAP Plumbing QLD

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-selected Next.js 16, TypeScript, regular Git commits, production deployment to Vercel project `asap-plumbing-website`. Do not use GPT Sites.

## Users

Brisbane homeowners arranging plumbing repairs, drain clearing, or a renovation. The user delegated prioritisation and design decisions: “Use your judgment”.

## Product Purpose

Introduce ASAP Plumbing QLD, show its actual work, and make it easy to call Mike or prepare an email enquiry. Primary success is a relevant customer contacting the business.

## Positioning

Local licensed plumber working in central Brisbane, with renovations and general plumbing explicitly described on the business's Facebook page. Direct contact with Mike is supported by a project post.

## Operating Context

Many customers will be on mobile, seeking urgent help or comparing renovation trades. Phone access must stay prominent. Google Maps lists the business as open 24 hours; arrival time and availability must be confirmed by phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Show services, real project imagery, service area information, FAQ, phone and email contacts, and links to the public profiles. An enquiry form can compose an email without claiming to have sent or stored the enquiry. Do not claim a working backend unless one is configured.

The user permits assumed placeholder business information and AI-generated images where photos are unsuitable. Keep assumed service descriptions documented; never invent customer testimonials, ratings, licence numbers, years of operation, or arrival guarantees.

## Brand Commitments

Business name: ASAP Plumbing QLD. Existing marketing uses orange, charcoal/black, white, heavy condensed lettering, and “Fast. Reliable. Done right.” The user selected a visual-concept-first workflow.
